RAMBO (Chapter 9)

THE GODS FIND THE SITUATION AMUSING

Sifu said the Gods must have found Rambo's situation quite amusing, as they performed something totally beyond our imagination and comprehension.

The Gods had suspended Rambo's cancer, stopping it from torturing him any further. The power of the Gods and the life force of Sifu had jointly entered Rambo's body, and Rambo definitely behaved like a dog with no cancer.

神可能覺得情況很有趣

師父說:神可能覺得情況很有趣,在這事上稍為顯現了我們完全想像不到的神力 – 祂們壓抑了 Rambo 的癌症, 令這病不再折磨他, 令他不用再那麼辛苦.

神的力量和師父的氣同時進入了 Rambo 的體內, 以致 Rambo 完全不像一隻患有癌症的狗.
