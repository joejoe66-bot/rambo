RAMBO (Chapter 8)

Sifu Transferred Energy To Rambo

Before the Vet arrived, Sifu dropped by to visit Rambo for the first time. Rambo was lying on the floor, motionless, except for some slight movements of the head, and practically an animal that was weakly struggling on the verge of death.

Casting a glance at Rambo, Sifu’s first and only words were “no death energy yet….”. Then Sifu put his hand about 2 feet above Rambo's head for no more than 2 mins, transferring energy to Rambo & reporting Rambo’s immediate death to the Gods at the same time.

Within a few minutes, everyone was stunned to see that Rambo actually started to gulp down water in his bowl quickly by himself (syringe not needed). A few hours later, Rambo even consumed a great bowl of rice with diced meat comfortably but eagerly all on his own (no help on blended food).

師父把他的氣及能量傳給 Rambo

在獸醫到達前，師父第一次來探 Rambo。師父一入門口，就見到 Rambo 躺在地上。基本上，Rambo 除了頭部還可以有微小動作之外，已經不能動彈，事實上就是一隻在死亡邊緣虛弱地彌留的動物。

師父看了Rambo 一眼，只講了一句說話，「還沒有死氣.…」，之後就把手放在Rambo 頭頂，距離不到 2 呎的距離，一面向大神報告 Rambo 的瀕死狀態，亦同時把能量傳入 Rambo 體內，前後不到 2 分鐘的過程。

幾分鐘之內，大家非常驚訝的發現，Rambo 快速地、大口大口地自行從它的碗裏面飲水 (不用針筒)；數小時後，更不可思議地、輕鬆熱切的進食一大碗米飯及小粒肉片，而不用再進食攪爛的糊狀食物。
