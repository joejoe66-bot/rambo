RAMBO (Chapter 11)

Rambo Was Miraculously Revived!

Rambo actively lived for another 6 weeks upon the power and energy of the Gods and Sifu – this is equivalent to about 11 months in human life.

Twice a week in each of those 6 weeks, I was still going with Rambo and his brother, Rocky, to hiking and picnicking, playing games such as ball-catching competition. From the videos, we can clearly see that Rambo was still every bit as bright and alert as Rocky, and in no way did he resemble a cancer patient who should have been dead by now.

Rambo 神奇地起死回生了!

Rambo 活潑地用神及師父的力量, 額外生存了6星期, 即相等於人類約11個月的生命.

在那 6 星期裏, 我繼續每星期 2 次帶 Rambo 及他的弟弟 Rocky 四處去行山和遊山玩水, 2兄弟又玩競逐追波遊戲等, 在以下的錄影帶裏, 大家可以看見Rambo 表現機警, 反應靈活, 跑得好快, 完全不像一個應該已經死去的癌症病人的模樣, 簡直就是一隻活潑正常的狗一樣.
