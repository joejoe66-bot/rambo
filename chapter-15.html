RAMBO (Chapter 15)

RAMBO DIED IN FRAGILITY AT HOME

Rambo died naturally in fragility at home in mid Oct 2007, after the energy transferred from Sifu had been used up and that there was no more life force remaining in him.

However, Rambo did not appear to be tortured by the cancer as he was previously – he could still drink water by himself, not much bleeding from nose & mouth, and with a low level of breathing difficulties as that of a regular dying person.

In conclusion, Rambo didn't die like a dog with cancer at all. .

Rambo 衰弱地在家裏死亡

當師父給 Rambo 的生命力全部用完的時候, Rambo 便很衰弱地、自然地在家裏死亡. 但 Rambo 死前仍然可以自己飲水, 口鼻只流很少量的血, 臨死前呼吸雖然有些不暢順, 但看上去較普通垂危病人的情況還要舒適. 總結來說, Rambo 絕對不像是一隻死於癌症的狗.
