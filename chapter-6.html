RAMBO (Chapter 6)

Rambo's Critical Symptoms of Cancer

  • a) Loss of appetite;
  • b) Great loss of weight;
  • c) Couldn't breathe because blood was blocking throat, nose & stomach;
  • d) Couldn't sleep because couldn't breathe;
  • e) Limbs were swollen, became a few times larger than normal due to infection and zero immunity ability, leading to loss of mobility;
  • f) Followed by 2 fingers being amputated at a later stage;
  • g) Couldn't eat blended food or drink water, not even with a syringe – choked on blood every time he tried to swallow something.

Rambo 之嚴重癌症病徵

  • a) 沒有胃口;
  • b) 體重大量減輕;
  • c) 內出血導致口鼻喉嚨被血所封, 以致呼吸極度困難;
  • d) 呼吸困難, 不能入睡;
  • e) 腿及手部受細菌感染, 沒有免疫能力, 腫脹了數倍, 不能行走;
  • f) 2隻手指後來要被割除;
  • g) 最終因為口鼻大量出血, 而不能嚥下任何包括糊狀的食物, 甚至用針筒打些清水入口也令他大量咳血.
