Previous Critical Symptoms of Cancer Disappeared

  • a) Bleeding from nose, mouth & anus completely stopped;
  • b) The Vet confirmed that the egg-sized lymph node tumors on both sides of throat (initially 7-8 lumps on each side), shoulders & legs were reduced in number & size, & became much softer;
  • c) Blood test showed that new red blood cells were made;
  • d) Resumed solid food daily with huge appetite.

  • a) 口鼻及肛門完全停止流血;

  • c) 驗血報告顯示有新的紅血球出現;
  • d) 每天自行進食大量固體食物, 胃口極佳.
